Waypoint 01 / 11

Introduction

Wordbloom: Secret Gardens ("the app," "we," "us") is a word-search game in which you find hidden words to restore five overgrown secret gardens. The app has no user accounts and no sign‑in. Every puzzle, your garden progress, and your seed and dewdrop balances are generated and stored entirely on your device — there is no developer server and finishing a level never requires the internet.

This Privacy Policy explains what the app stores on your device, what the Google services it uses for advertising, consent and purchases may collect automatically, and the choices available to you. It applies to the app as distributed on Google Play and is effective as of 22 September 2026. By installing or using Wordbloom: Secret Gardens, you agree to the practices described here.

Waypoint 02 / 11

Information we collect

The app collects no personal information and runs no developer server. It stores your progress only on your device, and the Google services it uses for ads, consent and billing collect a limited set of data automatically as part of their normal operation.

Garden progress (on‑device)

Which gardens and levels you've completed, stars earned, your Daily Bloom streak, your seed and dewdrop balances, unlocked postcards, and which garden pieces you've restored. This is written to a local save file on your device before each celebration animation plays, and is never uploaded.

Your nickname (on‑device)

An optional display name you can set or change in your Profile. It is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere — there's no account for it to be attached to.

Settings (on‑device)

Text size, haptics strength, sound and music toggles, keep‑awake, language, your personalised‑ads choice and your usage‑stats choice. All saved locally so the app remembers your preferences between sessions.

Purchase records (on‑device)

If you buy Remove Ads or a seed/dewdrop bundle, Google Play Billing shares a purchase token and product id, which the app stores locally so it knows what you own. The app never sees or stores your payment card details — Google Play handles all payment processing.

Advertising data

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device and app signals, coarse (IP‑derived) location, and ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks.

Consent choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) records your ad‑consent choice on your device so it can be respected and changed later.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts or sign‑in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or calendar. It does not use the camera or microphone, and it does not access your photos or files. Your garden progress and nickname are never transmitted to us or to any third party. There is a Usage Stats toggle in Settings → Privacy, off by default — turning it on currently sends nothing anywhere, because no analytics service is included in this version of the app (see "Policy changes" below).

Waypoint 03 / 11

How we use information

  • Gameplay — generating puzzles, tracking the words you've found, and saving your progress so you can resume exactly where you left off, even after closing the app.
  • Garden restoration — remembering which garden pieces you've bought with seeds and dewdrops so your gardens stay restored between sessions.
  • Profile & preferences — remembering your nickname, language, text size, haptics, sound and other settings so the app behaves consistently.
  • Advertising — showing ads between levels in the free version through AdMob, subject to your consent choices where applicable.
  • Purchases — selling and restoring seed/dewdrop bundles and the one‑time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play, and hiding ads once Remove Ads is active.

Waypoint 04 / 11

Third‑party services & data sharing

We don't sell your information and we don't operate a backend. The app integrates a small set of Google services for ads, consent and billing. Each provider receives only the data it needs to do its job and handles it under its own policy.

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

Serves and measures ads in the free version. May process your Advertising ID, IP address, device/app signals, coarse location and ad interactions. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Shows the ad‑consent form in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland and records your choice so it can be respected and changed later. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing

Processes seed/dewdrop bundles and the Remove Ads purchase. All payment details are handled by Google Play — the app never receives or stores card data, only a purchase token and product id. Governed by the Google Play Terms of Service.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

Waypoint 05 / 11

App permissions

Wordbloom's own gameplay code needs no special permissions at all. The only permissions in the app come from the Google SDKs that power ads and purchases. You can review app permissions at any time in Android Settings.

  • Internet & network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used only by the Google SDKs (ads, purchases) and to show the offline notice in the Shop. Puzzle generation, gameplay, saving, and the Garden Journey, Daily Bloom, Riddle Garden and Zen Pond modes never use the network.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — added automatically by the ads SDK for ad delivery and measurement.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, SMS, call log, storage/files, or location permissions.

Waypoint 06 / 11

Advertising & your consent choices

The free version of Wordbloom shows interstitial ads served by Google AdMob, and only between levels — at most once every 3 levels and at least 4 minutes apart. Ads never appear in the first 6 levels, on Home, in Zen Pond, during Restoration, on a Chapter Reveal, while you're offline, or once you've bought Remove Ads. Every ad break shows a persistent "AD" label, a short countdown, and a close button.

  • Rewarded video is always optional — offered from the Shop, the extra‑hint sheet and the Level Complete screen, clearly labelled "OPTIONAL AD," and always shown next to a "No thanks" option of equal weight. It states the exact reward before you watch it.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown before any personalised ads. You can choose personalised or non‑personalised ads; your choice is saved and re‑applied on future launches. You can reopen the form any time from Settings → Privacy.
  • Anywhere, you can limit ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (reset or delete your Advertising ID). Personalised ads stay off by default until you turn them on.
  • Buying Remove Ads (a one‑time purchase billed through your Google Play account) removes all ads permanently. Use "Restore purchases" in the Shop or Settings if you reinstall or switch devices.

Waypoint 07 / 11

Storage, security & retention

Your garden progress, nickname, settings and purchase records are stored in a single local save file in the app's private storage on your device. Each found word is written to that file before its celebration animation plays, and the app keeps a backup copy of the save so a crash mid‑write can't lose your progress. There is no cloud storage and no developer database.

Backup is not available in this version of the app. Your garden progress, purchases and settings are not currently backed up off your device — if you uninstall the app or lose your device, that local save cannot be recovered. We plan to offer an optional backup in a future update and will update this policy first.

Retention. Your local save stays on your device until you use Delete my data in Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall. Your Remove Ads entitlement is also recorded by Google Play against your Google account, so restoring it after a reinstall doesn't depend on the local save. Data handled by Google — advertising identifiers and purchase records — is retained under Google's own policies.

Waypoint 08 / 11

Your rights & choices

Wherever you live, the app gives you direct control. Depending on your region (for example, GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may also have statutory rights to access or delete personal data held about you.

Delete your data with Settings → Delete my data. The confirmation sheet names exactly what goes (gardens, levels, stars, streak, seeds & dewdrops, nickname), puts "Keep it" first, and needs a second tap. Your Remove Ads purchase, receipts and app settings are kept.

Change your nickname at any time from your Profile.

Control ad personalisation via Settings → Privacy and Android's Ads settings.

Restore purchases from the Shop or Settings if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

Adjust accessibility settings — text size, haptics strength, colour palette, reduce motion and language — from Settings at any time.

Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.

For data the app stores, the in‑app controls above are the fastest way to exercise your rights. For data held by Google (advertising, purchases), use Google's account and privacy tools, or contact us and we will help direct your request.

Waypoint 09 / 11

Children's privacy

Wordbloom: Secret Gardens is a general‑audience word game, is not directed at children under 13, and is not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate action.
